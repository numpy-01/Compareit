How is Vehicle IDV Determined?

IDV (Insured Declared Value) represents the maximum current market value of your vehicle that the insurer will pay out in the event of total loss or theft.

Motor Insurance Claim & Buying Guide

What is Claim Settlement Ratio (CSR)?

CSR represents the percentage of claims paid out against total claims received by an insurer in a financial year as published by IRDAI. Ratios exceeding 96% reflect superior claim reliability.

Can I transfer my NCB when switching insurers?

Yes! NCB belongs to you (the vehicle owner), not the insurance company. You can transfer up to 50% NCB bonus seamlessly to any insurer by producing your renewal notice or NCB retention letter.

Why buy directly on the insurer's portal?

Direct company portals eliminate third-party commission surcharges and grant direct access to the insurer's mobile app claims network, roadside tracking, and dedicated claim officers.
